Welcome to Villa Belvedere

One farm, five centuries, one oil

On the slopes of Monte Pisano, between Pisa and Lucca, stands Villa Belvedere: an old Tuscan farmhouse with a dovecote tower, surrounded by olive terraces that look out over the plain and, on clear days, all the way to the sea.

From 1536 this land belonged to the olive mill and the palazzo of the Count of Pisa. Generations of sharecroppers – the mezzadri – pruned, harvested and carried the olives down to the valley. Today we continue that work organically, with the same respect for the hill.
