Welcome to Villa Belvedere

One farm, five centuries, one oil

On the slopes of Monte Pisano, between Pisa and Lucca, stands Villa Belvedere: an old Tuscan farmhouse with a dovecote tower, surrounded by olive terraces that look out over the plain and, on clear days, all the way to the sea.

From 1536 the house was part of the estates of the Counts Agostini Fantini Venerosi della Seta. Around 1735 the sharecroppers – the mezzadri – began clearing the forest and planting olive trees, each one protected by a crescent-shaped stone wall, the “lunetta”. Some of those trees are now around 300 years old.

Today we farm the grove organically and press in our own olive mill: from harvest to bottle, everything happens here, with the same respect for the hill.
