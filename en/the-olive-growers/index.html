Who we are

Ralph and Christina Striewski

In 2024 we left [place/region of origin] and moved to Belvedere. We were won over by the evening light on the olive trees, the silence of the terraces and the feeling of stepping into a story far bigger than ourselves.

[Short personal introduction: previous careers, what brought you to Italy, your bond with the land.]

We were not born farmers: we have learned – and learn every day – from our neighbours, from those who tended the grove before us and from the trees themselves. With them, we carry on work that began almost three hundred years ago, when the mezzadri planted the first olive trees.

What matters to us

Respect for the hill

We look after the dry-stone walls, let grass grow between the rows and protect the bees, insects and birds that live in the grove.

Uncompromising quality

Early harvest, immediate milling in our own mill, laboratory analysis: we want to know exactly what is in our oil.

A shared story

We tell the story of Belvedere and the people who lived here – because a landscape is memory.

The calendar

A year in the olive grove

  1. January – March

    Pruning

    We thin the crowns so that light and air can reach inside the tree.

  2. April – May

    Flowering

    The trees are covered in tiny white flowers – the most delicate moment.

  3. June – August

    Fruit set

    The fruit forms and grows; we tend the soil and the walls.

  4. September

    Waiting

    We watch the olives ripen and prepare nets, crates and tools.

  5. October – November

    Harvest and milling

    We pick as the olives turn colour and press them the same day in our own mill.

  6. December

    Rest

    The trees rest. We taste the new oil and give thanks to the hill.

The olive trees of Belvedere

Our grove has around 2,300 trees on approx. 7.5 hectares of terraces, between 100 and 160 metres above sea level. Some trees are around 300 years old: the mezzadri planted them in their lunette, and they will bear fruit long after we are gone.

When we bought the Villa and the grove in 2024, there was – and still is – a good deal of work to do before every tree can be harvested again. Together with the Azienda Agricola Villa Belvedere di Corliano we took over the organic certification and the estate’s own olive mill, so the whole process, from harvest to bottle, takes place here on our land.
